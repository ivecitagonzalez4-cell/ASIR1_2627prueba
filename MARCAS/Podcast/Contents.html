Is Your Identity Really Safe?

Artificial intelligence is changing the world. However, this technology also creates new dangers for our privacy and personal security.

Criminals can use AI to copy faces, imitate voices and steal personal information. These techniques make it easier to impersonate other people online.

The New Threats of Artificial Intelligence

Discover three ways criminals can use AI to steal identities.

Voice Cloning

Artificial intelligence can imitate someone's voice using audio recordings. Criminals may use it to make fake phone calls and deceive families.

Identity Fraud

Deepfake Videos

Deepfakes are manipulated videos or images that make people appear to say or do things they never actually did.

Fake Identity

Personal Data Theft

Criminals can obtain personal information and use it to impersonate someone, access accounts or commit fraud.

Data Theft

How Does an AI Scam Work?

A criminal can use artificial intelligence to create a convincing fake identity.

01

Collect Information

Criminals obtain photos, videos or voice recordings from the internet.

02

Use Artificial Intelligence

AI tools generate a fake voice, image or video to imitate another person.

03

Deceive the Victim

The criminal contacts someone and tries to obtain money or personal information.

AI Identity Theft: Risks and Solutions

Different threats require different security measures.

Type of Attack Possible Consequences Security Measure
Voice Cloning Fake emergency calls and financial scams. Verify the caller using another phone number or a family verification code.
Deepfakes Fake videos, misinformation and impersonation. Check the original source before trusting or sharing videos.
Data Theft Unauthorized access to accounts and personal data. Use strong passwords and two-factor authentication.
Phishing Stolen passwords and sensitive information. Avoid suspicious links and verify websites.
