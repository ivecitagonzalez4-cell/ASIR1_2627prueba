01 • The Threat

Biometric Theft

Every social media video or voicemail gives away our vocal pitch and facial geometry.

With just 3 seconds of audio, generative AI can clone a voice or fake a live video call.

02 • The Impact

Real-World Risks

Hackers bypass bank voice verification systems to authorize fraudulent transfers.

In the workplace, criminals impersonate executives on Zoom to request urgent money wires.

03 • The Defense

Protection Tips

Set up a secret family safe word for emergencies and tighten social media privacy.

Avoid voice-only bank logins and always verify urgent calls on a trusted number.

Real Case Study

Special Moments & Anecdote

Highlights from Ived's real-life experience with AI identity theft

The Audio Trap

Attackers scraped Ived's voice from a public webinar he spoke at months earlier.

Free and accessible AI tools cloned his voice in seconds without needing a studio.

The Fake Emergency

Scammers called Ived's brother claiming he had a car accident and needed urgent cash.

The clone copied his exact cadence, pauses, and even the way he breathes when stressed.

The Aftermath

Criminals also tried to use that same voice clip to access his financial accounts.

The biggest impact was psychological: his family now hesitates every time he calls.
